CHOPPAH 1.4.1: the breaks play as the record.

- **Slices in order sound like the original break.** Each slice used to jump to its marker on its step, and the markers sit on the hits, often 10 to 30 ms off the grid: every jump skipped a bit of the break or played a hit twice. Now, when the sound playing is already where the next slice begins, it plays on, so 1 to 16 is the break as it was recorded, its groove and all. Played out of order, a slice still starts on its hit.
- **Each marker lands on its own hit.** A marker now moves at most a third of a 16th, so it no longer grabs the hit a 16th away (the Amen's slices 5, 7 and 13 and the Funky Drummer's slice 8 did). The same on the breaks page: send your own break again to get the new markers.

Saved settings and patterns from 1.4 load as they were.
